Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What we collect

Travial collects your email address for authentication, and trip data (bookings, expenses, itineraries) that you choose to enter. We also collect basic usage analytics to improve the app.

How we use it

Your data is used solely to provide the Travial service. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data storage

Your data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure. We use industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest.

Third-party services

Travial uses Google Gemini for AI features and Google Maps for location data. These services have their own privacy policies.

Your rights
